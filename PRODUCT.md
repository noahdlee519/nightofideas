# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS with no build step, deployed on Vercel at nightofideas.dev (domain already purchased on Vercel). Chosen by the club organizer so future members can edit it without a framework.

## Users

- **Prospective presenters:** University of Chicago students (and professors) from any department who have an original idea, philosophical or otherwise, and are deciding whether to apply to give a talk.
- **Prospective audience:** Students across the university deciding whether to come to the next Night (typically 60+ attend).
- **Faculty:** Professors who may give talks alongside students.

## Product Purpose

Night of Ideas is a University of Chicago club affiliated with the Philosophy Department. Each quarter the host vets applications and selects six students to deliver lightning-round talks about an original idea. The reviewing team workshops and edits each accepted talk with the student for several weeks before the Night itself, later in the quarter. High-profile professors often give talks too. Dinner is always provided.

The site succeeds when a curious student leaves having applied to present (Google Form) or having decided to come, and when past talks are easy to browse as proof of what a Night is like.

## Positioning

Not a lecture series and not an open mic: every talk is an original idea from a member of the university, selected and then workshopped for weeks with an editorial team, delivered as a lightning round, on a single night, over dinner.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Past talks link out to their slide decks (Google Slides / Drive). The site does not host slides.
- No CMS or backend. Content lives in the HTML/JS and is edited by hand.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Night of Ideas".

## Product Principles

1. The idea is the headline: talks and their authors carry the page, not the club.
2. Apply is the one action that matters; attending is the second.
3. Show real past work rather than describing what a Night is like.
4. Open to everyone: no department, year, or philosophy background is required.
5. Quarter-specific facts live in one place (the apply section) and are edited each quarter. The organizer chose to say that applications are open for the current quarter, with no date until one is set.
