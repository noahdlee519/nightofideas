# Home page (index.html)

Scope: the single-page site at nightofideas.dev. Visitor mode: **Persuade**.

- Audience: UChicago students (and faculty) deciding whether to apply to present, or to come.
- Job: understand what a Night is in seconds, see real past talks, apply via the Google Form.
- Proof: five real past talks with their title slides and slide links; faculty speaker Agnes Callard; the real mechanism (six selected, weeks of workshopping, lightning talks, 60+ attend, dinner).
- Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, no build; no date or venue shown; nothing invented (quotes, dates, attendance beyond "sixty or more").
- Build path: code-led (no image generation in this environment). Direction roll ran degraded (no challengers; roll service unreachable).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Night of Ideas is a juried Salon held at night. Each quarter the jury admits six works (talks) to the hang, and the site is that Salon's livret: numbered entries, cartels, règlement, hung frame-to-frame. It refuses the event-landing default of hero + date + three icon steps + card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Tanner nocturne ground (deep blue-green night, #0a1215), moonlit ivory text, gilt (burnished gold with per-side lit/shaded moldings), and one full-bleed claret Salon wall for the hang. Bodoni Moda display at high optical size, Old Standard TT for livret text, tracked Bodoni capitals for cartels. No mono, no sans, no cards: frames, cartels, catalogue numbers, articles.

STORY: The visitor arrives at night on a rooftop (Tanner's Nicodemus), reads the name as the moon waxes into its O, understands "six students, six original ideas, one night, dinner," walks the wall of past works, reads the règlement, and applies under the Annunciation's column of light.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed Nicodemus nocturne, graded toward night. The NIGHT / (moon)F / IDEAS lockup set at poster scale in the left column (about 40% of the width, so Tanner's lit figure holds the right), the moon, a real lunar photograph, waxing into its O on load. Beneath, the livret title line, a short three-sentence offer, and the primary "Apply to present" (flat gilt) with "Walk the past talks" beside it. A museum cartel credits the painting bottom-right.

FORM: The Salon Catalogue (my list position 1; presented as IMPECCABLE'S PICK and chosen by the user over the assigned Night Atlas, position 7). Seed key 137881fd. Signature interaction: the Hang, a draggable, spring-damped gallery wall of framed title slides where a picture light follows the active work and its cartel re-engraves; a numbered livret index drives it too. Motion grammar: slow lamp-lit reveals (clip-path unveilings, blur-to-focus type), a nav moon whose phase tracks scroll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Contract amendments (builder decisions, recorded after finish review round 1)

- Lockup moved from full width to a left-column poster scale: at full width it covered the painting's figure, which is the scene's argument (a conversation by night).
- Offer is three short sentences (six talks, the workshop, sixty listeners and dinner); each carries a distinct product fact from PRODUCT.md.
- Materials re-derived from photographs: frames are 9-slice rasters cut from a CC0 Met Louis XIV-style carved frame (ebony variant is a tonal derivative); the moon is NASA LRO imagery (public domain); button, rail and hook are flat gilt.
